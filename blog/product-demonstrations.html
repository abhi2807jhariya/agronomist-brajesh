Product Demonstrations & Field Trials

Proving agrochemical efficacy, crop response, and farmer profitability right on the ground.

Illustrative field-work imagery: Side-by-side result demonstration on farmer fields.

Seeing is Believing in the Field

In agricultural inputs, true farmer trust is built when results are visible in the soil and foliage. Product demonstrations bridge technical agronomic science with real-world farming outcomes.

Through carefully organized comparative plots (Treated vs. Farmer's Practice), we showcase the distinctive performance of crop protection products, biostimulants, and specialized nutritional formulations. These trials give farmers firsthand confidence before they invest for the entire season.

Working with local distributors and retail partners, field trials also create strong pull-demand at the retail counter, accelerating liquidation and brand reputation in the territory.

Demonstration Methodology

01 Plot Selection

Select roadside progressive farmer fields with uniform soil and active pest or weed pressure.

02 Precision Spraying

Ensure calibrated spray volume (150–200 L/acre), correct nozzle type (flat fan / hollow cone), and accurate dosage.

03 Field Days & Visits

Gather 25–40 neighboring farmers at peak efficacy window (7–14 days post-application) to evaluate differences.

04 Harvest & Feedback

Record yield advantages, calculate economic return on investment (ROI), and collect authentic testimonials.

Agronomist’s Perspective: Good Demonstration Practices

A successful demonstration is never just about applying chemical inputs — it requires educating farmers on water quality (pH), optimal spray timing (early morning or late evening), correct staging of weeds and pests, and thorough foliage coverage. This scientific precision is what transforms a simple trial into long-term commercial loyalty.
